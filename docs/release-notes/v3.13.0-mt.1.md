# v3.13.0-mt.1

The first Tiger Multi-tools release: the TigerScale V3 firmware v3.13.0 plus a start menu of tools,
and you choose which ones the menu shows, and in which order. This is an unofficial fork, not
published or supported by the TigerTag Project.

## Added

- **Start menu.** The scale boots on a list of tools instead of going straight to weighing. Tiger
  Scale (spool weighing, unchanged) is always first; Settings > Tools reopens the list.
- **TigerPOD.** While the tool is open, the scale's two readers serve Tiger Studio on the same
  network the way a TigerPOD does: read and write chips from Studio (needs the Studio build of
  this fork).
- **Parcel scale.** Live weight in grams with TARE, no account and no network. Needs the parcel
  accessory, printed separately.
- **Calibration & test.** Raw load-cell readings, their spread and rate, a verdict naming the
  likely wiring fault, and the calibration wizard.
- **Heat press memo.** Temperatures and times per product, sorted by category with thumbnails, a
  timer with an end alarm, and an editor in the browser at `http://<scale>/presse`.
- **Your tools, your order.** The eye button of the start menu shows or hides each tool and moves
  it up or down. The same choice is at `http://<scale>/tools`, and the installer page sends it over
  USB right after a flash. Keep only Tiger Scale and the scale boots straight to weighing.
- Every button clicks on the speaker when touched (follows mute and volume).

## Changed

- Updates now come from this fork's own site, so an official TigerScale release is never offered as
  an update and cannot remove the tools.

## Fixed

- The ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-3.5 (without B) makes sound: its speaker amplifier was never switched on.
- The scale reconnects to Wi-Fi on its own after a drop, and comes online when it booted with the
  router down.

## Known issues

- A scale on the official firmware does not see this release in Settings > Update: its updates
  come from the official site. Install it once over USB from the installer page; updates then
  come from this fork.

## Updating

Over the air from **Settings > Update** on a scale already running Tiger Multi-tools, or from the
installer page (https://juditech3d.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/) over USB. Answer no when asked to
erase the device: that keeps your Wi-Fi credentials, TigerTag session and calibration.
