# v3.13.0-mt.5

Two new tools in the start menu: a parts counter and resin mixing.

## Added

- **Parts counter.** A step-by-step wizard:
  1. put the empty counting tray on and press TARE;
  2. put 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50 or 100 reference parts and press Confirm;
  3. check: add as many parts again, and the scale should show twice the number (green when it
     does). *Correct, count* takes the part weight again over all of them, for a reference twice
     as precise; *Redo* goes back to step 2;
  4. count: add or remove parts, the count follows.

  The part weight is kept: next time, *Same parts* goes straight to counting. Under 10 g of
  reference, the screen advises taking more parts. Needs a counting tray on the scale, printed
  separately.
- **Resin mixing.** Two-part resin or epoxy by weight: set the ratio as "100 : B" (kept for next
  time), put the cup on the parcel-scale adapter and press TARE, pour part A, then *Go to B*: the
  screen shows the B to add, what is in and what is left, with a bar, two beeps when the dose is
  reached (within 2 %) and red when it is over.
- Both tools compute on the filtered weight without the display's 1 g dead zone, so a count or a
  dose is not off by a gram.

## Known issues

- The counting tray and the parcel-scale adapter are not published yet.
- A scale on the official firmware does not see this release in Settings > Update: install it once
  over USB from the installer page; updates then come from this fork.

## Updating

Over the air from **Settings > Update** on a scale already running Tiger Multi-tools (from
v3.13.0-mt.4 on, the start menu also offers it), or from the installer page
(https://juditech3d.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/) over USB. Answer no when asked to erase the device:
that keeps your Wi-Fi credentials, TigerTag session and calibration. The new tools appear at the
end of the start menu; move or hide them with the eye button.
