# v3.13.0-mt.6

Bambu Lab spools in TigerPOD mode, and no more screen sleep in the middle of a weighing.

## Added

- **Bambu Lab chips in the TigerPOD tool.** Put a Bambu Lab spool on one of the scale's readers
  while TigerPOD is open: the scale opens the chip (MIFARE Classic, each sector with the key
  derived from its UID) and sends its data to Tiger Studio, which shows the filament: type,
  colour, weight. The chip is only read, never written. TigerTag chips work exactly as before.
  Needs a Tiger Studio that decodes Bambu Lab chips.

## Fixed

- **The screen no longer sleeps during a weighing.** Screen sleep paused the whole weighing: a
  spool put down just before the sleep delay ran out stayed half-weighed and was not synced until
  someone touched the screen. Now a spool on the platform whose weighing is not finished keeps the
  screen on, and the sleep delay starts again when the weighing ends. A spool left on the platform
  after it has been sent still lets the screen sleep.

## Known issues

- The Tiger Scale weigh screen itself does not recognise Bambu Lab chips yet: only the TigerPOD
  tool does.
- A scale on the official firmware does not see this release in Settings > Update: install it once
  over USB from the installer page; updates then come from this fork.

## Updating

Over the air from **Settings > Update** on a scale already running Tiger Multi-tools (the start
menu also offers it), or from the installer page (https://juditech3d.github.io/Tiger-Scale-V3/)
over USB. Answer no when asked to erase the device: that keeps your Wi-Fi credentials, TigerTag
session and calibration.
